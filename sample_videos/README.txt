Place demo mining-site video clips here (e.g. mine.mp4) to test the
"video" input mode:

    cd backend
    python app.py --source video --path ../sample_videos/mine.mp4

No video is bundled with this prototype (to keep the deliverable small and
avoid shipping third-party footage). Any short clip containing trucks,
cars, or people will work for a demo — dashcam or drone footage of a
quarry, construction site, or roadway all work well as stand-ins for a
mining haul road.

You can also just use --source webcam, or the "START SAFETY DEMO" button
in the dashboard, which runs a fully scripted Bailadila Monsoon Scenario
without needing any video file at all.
